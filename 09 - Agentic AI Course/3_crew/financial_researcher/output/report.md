**Company Overview**

### Company History

Microsoft was founded on April 4, 1975, by Bill Gates and Paul Allen. The company's first product was a version of the programming language BASIC for the Altair 8800 microcomputer. Since then, Microsoft has grown to become one of the world's largest and most successful technology companies. Microsoft went public in 1986 with an initial public offering (IPO) that raised $61 million.

### Current Status

As of 2023, Microsoft is a multinational technology company with a market capitalization of over $2 trillion.
